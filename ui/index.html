Give a world to a friend

Packs the world into one zip on your Desktop. Your character isn't included. Your friend installs it with "Install a world" below (or copies the .sav into their SaveGames folder) and hosts it.

Install a world from a friend

Pick the zip (or .sav) your friend sent. If you already have a world with the same name, it is backed up first. Quit the game before installing.
